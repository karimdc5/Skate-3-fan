    How to play

    📱 Touch

    • Left thumb: steer / spin in the air / walk
    • PUSH speed up · BRAKE slow down (powerslide when fast)
    • REVERT: tap as you land to spin back around
    • Right side: swipe for tricks
      • Tap or ↑ Ollie · ↖ Kickflip · ↗ Heelflip
      • ← Pop Shove-it · → FS Shove-it
      • ↙ 360 Flip · ↘ Laser Flip · Swipe ↓ and hold = Manual
    • GRAB in the air (let go before landing). Swipe while grabbing = Fingerflip. Hold GRAB and tap = Boneless.
    • Turn on Pro flick in Settings for every trick, Skate style.

    🎮 Controller (flick-it)

    • RS ↓ then flick ↑ Ollie · ↖ Kickflip · ↗ Heelflip
    • Flick super fast ↖/↗ = Double flips · flick ↑ fast = Impossible
    • ↓ → ← then ↖ Varial Kickflip · ↓ → → then ↗ Varial Heelflip
    • ↓ → → then ↖ Hardflip · ↓ → ← then ↗ Inward Heelflip
    • ↓ roll through ← to ↑ = 360 Flip · through → = Laser Flip
    • ↓ → ← / → and let go = Shove-its · ↓ → side → ↓ and let go = 360 Shove-it
    • Start from RS ↑ = Nollie · riding backwards = Fakie / Switch
    • Pull RS ↓ longer before flicking = higher pop
    • Hold RS ↓ softly = Manual · ↑ softly = Nose manual
    • A/✕ Push · X/□ Brake · B/○ Revert · Y/△ Off board
    • LT RT / L2 R2 Grabs (LS picks the grab) · LS picks grind type
    • D-pad ↑ Set marker · ↓ Go to marker · ← Bail · → Object dropper
    • View/Create Replay editor · Click LS Camera

    Skate 3 details

    • Land with the board lined up (forwards or fakie). Land half-turned and you bail. Barely finish a flip = Sketchy (half points).
    • Fly along tall walls for a Wallride, flick to Wallie off. Kinked rails and hubbas keep your grind going.
    • Named gaps: 5 Stair, Funbox Gap, Halfpipe Transfer, Hill Kicker Gap, Mega Ramp Gap.
    • Hall of Meat: every bail scores damage and broken bones. Bail on purpose with D-pad ←.
    • Game modes: Own the Spot, S.K.A.T.E., Hall of Meat and Death Race.

    Settings

    🎮 Controller Connect

    1. Turn the controller on by holding the Xbox button.
    2. Hold the small Pair button (top edge, near the USB port) until the Xbox logo flashes fast.
    3. On your iPhone open Settings → Bluetooth and tap Xbox Wireless Controller.
    4. Come back here and press any button on the controller.
    1. With the controller off, hold Create (left of the touchpad) and the PS button together.
    2. Let go when the light bar around the touchpad starts flashing.
    3. On your iPhone open Settings → Bluetooth and tap DualSense Wireless Controller.
    4. Come back here and press any button on the controller.

    Safari only shows a controller after you press a button while this page is open. Needs iOS 15 or newer. Already paired before? Just turn the controller on and press a button.
