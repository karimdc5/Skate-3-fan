    How to play

    📱 Touch

    • Left thumb: drag to steer / spin in the air
    • PUSH: speed up · BRAKE: slow down
    • Right side: swipe for tricks
      • Tap or ↑ Ollie · ↖ Kickflip · ↗ Heelflip
      • ← Pop Shove-it · → FS Shove-it
      • ↙ 360 Flip · ↘ Laser Flip
      • Swipe ↓ and hold = Manual
    • GRAB: hold in the air, let go before landing
    • Switch to Pro flick in Settings for the real Skate-style flick

    🎮 Controller (flick-it)

    • Left stick: steer, spin in air, pick grab/grind
    • Right stick: pull ↓ then flick ↑ = Ollie
    • ↓ then flick ↖ / ↗ = Kickflip / Heelflip
    • ↓ then roll through ← or → up = 360 Flip / Laser Flip
    • ↓ then ← or → and let go = Shove-its
    • Start ↑ instead of ↓ = Nollie version
    • Hold ↓ softly = Manual, hold ↑ = Nose manual
    • A / ✕ Push · X / □ Brake · LT RT / L2 R2 Grabs
    • Y / △ Back to marker · D-pad ↑ Set marker · B / ○ Camera

    ⌨️ Keyboard

    • WASD steer/push/brake · Space Ollie · Q Kickflip · E Heelflip
    • R Shove-it · F 360 Flip · Z/C Grab · T Marker · V Camera · Esc Pause

    Land with your board lined up with where you're going (forwards or fakie). Spin halfway and you'll bail. Grind by landing on rails, ledges and coping.

    Settings

    🎮 Controller Connect

    1. Turn the controller on by holding the Xbox button.
    2. Hold the small Pair button (top edge, near the USB port) until the Xbox logo flashes fast.
    3. On your iPhone open Settings → Bluetooth and tap Xbox Wireless Controller.
    4. Come back here and press any button on the controller.
    1. With the controller off, hold Create (left of the touchpad) and the PS button together.
    2. Let go when the light bar around the touchpad starts flashing.
    3. On your iPhone open Settings → Bluetooth and tap DualSense Wireless Controller.
    4. Come back here and press any button on the controller.

    Safari only shows a controller after you press a button while this page is open. Needs iOS 15 or newer. Already paired before? Just turn the controller on and press a button.
